Our privacy promises

MirrorMatch never sells your information, never shares it for advertising, and deletes your face data within 30 days of when you leave. This policy is a draft: have a privacy lawyer review it, and fill in the items in [BRACKETS], before it goes live.

In short, we promise to:

  • Never sell or rent your data, and never share it with advertisers, data brokers, or ad networks. The app contains no advertising trackers or pixels.
  • Ask before we touch your face. Face analysis only starts after you give separate written consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Keep the least we can. Driver's license and income documents are deleted within 72 hours of checking them. We keep only your date of birth, age and a "verified" mark, never your license number or address.
  • Never show your hidden scores. Your tier, attractiveness score and match scores are used inside the app and never shown to anyone, including you or other members.
  • Never reveal where you live. Your home location is never shown; others see only a rounded distance, or nothing if you hide it.
  • Delete fast when you leave. Your profile, photos, answers and messages are deleted within 30 days of closing your account, and from backups within 90 days.
  • Give everyone the same rights, in every state and country: see, download, correct and delete your data, free.

For comparison, other popular apps keep profile data for a year or more after you leave and share data with advertising partners. Tinder and Hinge keep profile data for 1 year after closure, and Bumble keeps core profile data for 6 years (Tinder, Hinge, Bumble).

3. Face data (biometric information)

We create face measurements from your photos only after you sign a separate written consent, and we destroy them within 30 days of when you leave. This section is our public retention schedule under the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA) and similar laws in Texas, Washington and other states.

What we create. A set of numbers describing the geometry of your face (a "face template"), made from your sign-up selfie and profile photos. We do not keep fingerprints, voice prints or eye scans.

Why we use it, and nothing else:

  1. Confirming your photos are really you, and that you're a real person.
  2. Calculating facial symmetry, which is one part of the hidden score used to order your picks.
  3. Finding people whose look resembles the types you like, and learning your type from your likes.
  4. Blocking people banned from MirrorMatch from joining again.

Consent. Before any face data is created, we tell you in writing what we collect, why, and how long we keep it, and ask you to agree with a separate tap. It is not buried in these terms. You can withdraw consent in Settings at any time. Because face matching is central to MirrorMatch, withdrawing consent closes your account.

We never:

  • sell, lease, trade or otherwise profit from your face data
  • share it with advertisers or data brokers
  • use it to identify you anywhere outside MirrorMatch
  • let any partner use it to train their own systems

We disclose it only with your consent, or when a law, a valid warrant or a subpoena requires it.

How long we keep it. We destroy your face template and verification selfie at the first of these:

  • 30 days after you close your account or withdraw consent
  • 3 years after your last use of MirrorMatch

One exception: if you are banned for a safety violation, we keep only your face template, used only to stop you rejoining, until 3 years after your last interaction with us. That is the maximum Illinois law allows.

Security. Face data is encrypted when stored and when sent. It is stored apart from your name and profile, and only a small number of staff can access it, with every access logged. We protect it at least as carefully as passwords and account numbers.

Illinois law lets people sue for $1,000 to $5,000 per violation, so we treat these rules as fixed (BIPA summary). By comparison, Tinder and Hinge use face data only for verification and delete it within 30 days (Tinder, Hinge). Facebook shut down its face recognition system and deleted its face templates (Sophos).

4. Sensitive information

Sensitive details are always optional (except gender, which is needed for matching), and we use them only to match you with the people you ask for. Sensitive details include gender identity, who you're interested in, race or ethnicity, religion, politics, income, kids and marriage history.

  • Your choice: every optional field offers "Prefer not to say". Skipping a field slightly lowers your match score with people who said that field matters to them, and members who require it won't see you.
  • Never in your score: race, ethnicity, religion, politics, sex and gender never raise or lower your hidden tier. They are used only as filters that members choose for themselves.
  • Never for ads: we never use sensitive details for advertising, and never infer them from your activity.
  • Consent: adding a sensitive detail to your profile is your consent for it to be shown on your profile and used for matching. Remove it at any time, and it is deleted from our systems within 30 days.

8. Location

We use your approximate location only to show nearby people and the Traveling tag, and we don't keep a history of where you've been.

  • Permission: collected only if you allow it in your phone's settings. You can turn it off any time; picks then use your home city.
  • Rounded: locations are rounded to about 1 km (0.6 miles) before storage. Members see distance in whole miles.
  • No history: each update replaces the last. We keep only your current approximate location and your home location.
  • Home location: set to your current city at sign-up. You can change it only to where you are now, once every 30 days, so the Traveling tag stays honest. It's never shown to anyone.
  • Photos: GPS data is removed from every photo before it's stored.

9. Safety, reports and law enforcement

People on our safety team, not just software, review serious reports before any ban, and we give police information only with valid legal process.

  • Messages: checked automatically before sending. A person reads a conversation only when someone in it reports it, or when the law requires it.
    • Staff access: a small number of admin and moderator accounts can open any profile to review photos and reports. These are separate staff accounts that can't date on MirrorMatch. They can't read face data or unreported chats, and every profile they open is logged.
  • Reports: we tell the reported member that their account was reviewed, but never who reported them.
  • Court documents: documents attached to a report, such as convictions for sexual offenses or violent crimes, are seen only by the safety team. If a document leads to a ban, we keep it with the ban record. If not, we delete it within 90 days.
  • Inaccurate-info reports: a penalty applies only after 3 different members report the same field within 90 days. It's removed once the field is fixed or verified.
  • Date feedback: negative feedback counts only after 2 different members give it.
  • Children: we report apparent child sexual exploitation to the NCMEC CyberTipline, as federal law requires (18 U.S.C. § 2258A), and keep the related records as that law requires.
  • Police requests: we need a subpoena, court order or warrant. We'll notify you first unless the law or a court forbids it, or a life is at risk. Emergency requests to prevent death or serious injury are reviewed case by case. [We'll publish a yearly count of government requests.]

11. Your rights and choices

Every member gets the same rights, free, wherever they live, including those under California's CCPA/CPRA, other US state privacy laws, and the GDPR and UK GDPR.

  • See and download: get a copy of your data in Settings, delivered within 30 days.
  • Correct: edit your profile any time, or ask us to fix anything else.
  • Delete: delete photos, answers or your whole account in Settings. Deletion follows section 10.
  • Withdraw consent: for face data (closes your account), sensitive details or location, any time.
  • Human review: of any automatic decision that limits your account (section 5).
  • Object or restrict: ask us to stop or limit a use of your data.
  • Appeal: if we deny a request, reply to our answer and a different team member will review it within 45 days. If that doesn't resolve it, you can contact your state attorney general or data protection authority.
  • No penalty: using these rights never affects your tier, picks or prices.

We may ask you to confirm your identity first, for example with a code sent to your phone. An authorized agent can make a request for you with your signed permission.

12. Security, children, transfers and changes

Security. Data is encrypted when stored and when sent. Staff get access only as their job needs, every access to face data and safety records is logged, and we test our security regularly. If a breach affects your data, we'll notify you and regulators without delay, within 72 hours of discovery where the law requires. No system is perfectly secure, so use a unique password and report anything suspicious.

Adults only. MirrorMatch is for people 18 and over. We check age at sign-up and may require ID. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it and delete its data immediately. Records we must keep under section 9 are the only exception.

Where data is stored. Data is stored in the United States (Ohio, through our hosting provider Supabase). For members in the EEA or UK, we use Standard Contractual Clauses or other approved safeguards.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. We don't track you across other sites or apps, so these signals are always honored.

Changes. We'll notify you in the app at least 30 days before any material change. We'll ask for new consent before using face or sensitive data in any new way.
