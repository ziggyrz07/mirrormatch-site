MirrorMatch · For businesses

Be where local couples plan their date night

MirrorMatch is a face-verified dating app launching in Central Wisconsin (Wausau, Mosinee and Stevens Point first). When two members match and plan a date, we suggest places to go. Your business can be one of them.

Ways to partner with us

Date Spots

Your restaurant, bar, coffee shop or activity appears when matched members tap "Suggest a date spot", clearly marked Sponsored and shown only to people in your area.

Date Night deals

Offer MirrorMatch couples a deal, like 15% off dinner or a free appetizer. When two members plan a date at your place, they get a single-use code to show your staff, so you can see exactly how many dates we send you.

Sponsored events

Host a singles night, trivia or a "first date's on us" weekend, promoted to members nearby.

Why it's different

  1. Real people, nearby. Every member is face-verified, and suggestions are shown by area, so you reach locals who are actually going out.
  2. The right moment. We don't show pop-up ads. Your listing appears when two people who just matched are choosing where to go.
  3. No tracking. We never share members' personal data with partners. You get counts of views, picks and redeemed codes, not people's information.
  4. Founding partners. We're starting with a small number of local businesses per town, at founding-partner rates.
